TWITTER

Twitter (X) is a global social media and microblogging platform where users post, interact with, and discover short text-based messages called "posts" or "tweets," along with images, videos, and links. It is widely known for real-time news updates, public discourse, and trending global conversations.

INSTAGRAM

Instagram is a popular social media platform and mobile application designed for sharing photos, short videos, and real-time updates. It allows users to capture, edit, and post visual media, engage with content through likes, comments, and direct messages, and connect with global audiences or personal networks.

YOUTUBE

youTube is the world’s largest online video-sharing and social media platform. It allows users to watch, like, comment on, and share videos, as well as create and upload their own content through personal channels.
